The name

Gea. Na. Vid.

Romansh, the fourth national language, for Yes, No and Blank. All three words come from the Romansh voting booklets.

Gea
Yes → entailment

„… recumondan cussegl federal e parlament da metter in Gea en l'urna …“

“… the Federal Council and Parliament recommend putting a Yes in the ballot box …”

Booklet of 12 June 1994
Na
No → contradiction

„Nus recumandain in NA a las chapellinas blaus.“

“We recommend a NO to the blue-helmet troops.” (referendum committee)

Booklet of 12 June 1994
Vid
Blank, empty → neutral

„… abitaziuns da vacanzas ch'èn savens vidas …“

“… holiday homes that mostly stand empty …”

Booklet of 25 June 1995

The trade-off

Under a ninth of the tokens. Better verdicts.

Apertus 70B GeaNaVid 0.965 with 1,461 tokens. Whole booklet 0.889.
Beginner task Claim + reference text: 0.977 (8B) and 0.980 (70B).

Test scores also decided which variants were kept, so we add checks not used for tuning: 546 development claims outside the tuning subset 0.971 (8B) / 0.971 (70B), and unseen booklets (synthetic hold-out; 8B in its second evaluation, first 0.845) 0.874 / 0.932 vs 0.665 / 0.872 whole booklet. 14 October 2026.

Evidence

Every Yes or No points to a line in the booklet.

Na contradiction · label 2
Claim (Italian): the reduced VAT rate falls from 2.5 to 2.4 per cent
„Der dafür geltende reduzierte Mehrwertsteuersatz steigt von 2,5 auf 2,6 Prozent; …“
25.09.2022 · DE · page 28 · AHV 21: additional financing by VAT
input tokens 2,794 output tokens 52 Apertus 8B
Quotes, not paraphrase The evidence is booklet sentences, verbatim, each with its PDF page, from the section the claim names, chosen after the verdict without another model call.
Evidence first, label second Apertus names the passages before it gives the verdict, in strict JSON.
Not voting advice GeaNaVid says what the booklet says. It never says how to vote.

OST case v1-1484 (development split), live demo, 4 October 2026. Scored by OST's own evaluation script, one of our quoted sentences lies in the gold passage for 96.9 % (8B) and 96.3 % (70B) of the 324 entailment/contradiction cases of the v1.1 test split.
