The name

Gea. Na. Vid.

Romansh, the fourth national language, for Yes, No and Blank. All three words come from the Romansh voting booklets.

Gea
Yes → entailment

„… recumondan cussegl federal e parlament da metter in Gea en l'urna …“

“… the Federal Council and Parliament recommend putting a Yes in the ballot box …”

Booklet of 12 June 1994
Na
No → contradiction

„Nus recumandain in NA a las chapellinas blaus.“

“We recommend a NO to the blue-helmet troops.” (referendum committee)

Booklet of 12 June 1994
Vid
Blank, empty → neutral

„… abitaziuns da vacanzas ch'èn savens vidas …“

“… holiday homes that mostly stand empty …”

Booklet of 25 June 1995

Romansh text from the aligned corpus VotingBooklets-v1 (Stüssi & Vamvas, SwissText 2026, CC BY 4.0); English glosses ours.

The trade-off

A tenth of the tokens. Better verdicts.

Apertus 8B GeaNaVid 0.957 with 2,662 tokens. Whole booklet 0.723 with 20,156.
Apertus 70B GeaNaVid 0.968 with 1,531 tokens. Whole booklet 0.890.
Beginner task Claim + reference text: 0.978 (8B) and 0.980 (70B).

OST MNLIoverSwissVotingBooklets v1.0, held-out test split: 497 pairs from 7 voting dates, settings chosen on development data. 95 % intervals about ±0.02 (±0.025 with repeated pairs resampled together). 4 October 2026.

Evidence

Every verdict points to a line in the booklet.

Na contradiction · label 2
Claim (Italian): the reduced VAT rate falls from 2.5 to 2.4 per cent
„Der dafür geltende reduzierte Mehrwertsteuersatz steigt von 2,5 auf 2,6 Prozent; …“
25.09.2022 · DE · page 28 · AHV 21: additional financing by VAT
input tokens 2,794 time 0.8 s Apertus 8B
Quotes, not paraphrase The evidence is the booklet sentence that decides, verbatim, with its PDF page, from the section the claim names, chosen after the verdict without another model call.
Evidence first, label second Apertus names the passages before it gives the verdict, in strict JSON.
Not voting advice GeaNaVid says what the booklet says. It never says how to vote.

OST v1.0 case v1-1484 (development split), live demo, 4 October 2026. On the test split the quoted sentence lies in OST's gold section for 86 % (8B) and 88 % (70B) of entailment/contradiction answers.
