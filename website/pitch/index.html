The name

Gea. Na. Vid.

Romansh, the fourth national language, for Yes, No and Blank. All three words come from the Romansh voting booklets.

Gea
Yes → entailment

„… recumondan cussegl federal e parlament da metter in Gea en l'urna …“

“… the Federal Council and Parliament recommend putting a Yes in the ballot box …”

Booklet of 12 June 1994
Na
No → contradiction

„Nus recumandain in NA a las chapellinas blaus.“

“We recommend a NO to the blue-helmet troops.” (referendum committee)

Booklet of 12 June 1994
Vid
Blank, empty → neutral

„… abitaziuns da vacanzas ch'èn savens vidas …“

The trade-off

4 % of the tokens. Better verdicts.

Apertus 8B Ladder 0.88 with 1,856 tokens. Whole booklet 0.60 with 40,149.
Apertus 70B Ladder 0.94 with 1,715 tokens. Whole booklet 0.85, and 2.8 s instead of 0.9 s.
Gold passage 0.94 and 0.93. Retrieval loses 0.06 with 8B and nothing with 70B.

Held-out synthetic test: 101 claims on four booklets (2010, 2016, 2020, 2025), labels by two other LLMs with cross-review, not yet checked by a human. 95 % intervals ±0.05–0.10. Measured 1 October 2026.

31 / 48

cross-lingual demo pairs where plain keyword search finds the right page in its top 5. Without help, 17 of 48 miss.

So GeaNaVid searches the booklet edition in the claim's language and maps the hits back by page. The right passage is then in the top 5 for 36 of 38 claims, with zero extra tokens.

Own probe: BM25 over the three editions of the 14 June 2026 booklet, 24 non-neutral demo claims × 2 other languages. Same-language recall@3 on the same claims: 22 / 24.

Quotes, not paraphrase Evidence is a real text block from the PDF, with page and position. The model picks it; it does not write it.
Evidence first, label second Apertus names the passages before it gives the verdict, in strict JSON.
Not voting advice GeaNaVid says what the booklet says. It never says how to vote.
